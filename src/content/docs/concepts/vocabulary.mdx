---
title: Vocabulary
description: The handful of words supervise uses, each with one meaning.
---

| Word           | Is                                                                                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Seat**       | an interactive `claude` hosted by `supervise claude`, under a name                         |
| **Master**     | the seat with a directory's master role; the other seats report to it                      |
| **Arm**        | lets a seat clear and hand off by itself, until disarmed or until a time                   |
| **Recap**      | the note a session leaves its successor, with a `next` step; `handoff: ready` makes it final |
| **Handoff**    | a ready recap, a `/clear`, and a successor that starts on the recap's `next`               |
| **Cairn**      | the directory that holds your projects' routing skills and notes                           |
| **Project**    | a repository supervise knows through a routing skill in the cairn                          |
| **Land grant** | a single-use permission to land one commit, given by a human or the repository's master    |

## Who may do what

Some verbs are a human's alone, whatever a session asks for: setting a
directory's master, and anything that widens what sessions may do. Arming is a
human's call or the master's. A seat can never arm, clear or type into itself.
